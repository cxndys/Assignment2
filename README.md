### How to use 

    It's similar like the usual mario game. Press start in the menu and select the level, wait for a few seconds, and the game scene will show up. At the end of the level, player score will be shown. Remaining time will be multiplied by 10 and added to the player score in total. 

    World Map: There are 2 world maps --> Level 1 and Level 2. 

    Player: Use ASWD or arrow keys to move and space to jump. 

    Enemies: Flower - If player makes contact with flower, life -1. Turtle - If player make contact, life -1. Player can stomp on turtle head and turtle will turn into shell. If player makes contact with shell, the shell will move, but it player won't lose life. 

    Quesion Blocks: There are 2 types. Mushroom - Player will become bigger. When player is big and it makes contact with enemy, the player will become small again but not lose life.  Coin - Player can collect coins. 1 coin adds 100 points to score

    Animations: Player walk and jump animations. Turtle shell animation. 

    Sound Effects: BGM starts playing in game scene (No BGM in Menu or LevelSelect). Player jump and die sound are implemented. There are also some other additional sound effects such as when mario interacts with question blocks. 

    UI: Player life (Player starts with 3), Timer (in seconds), Player score are all displayed on top of the screen.
